---
name: teach
description: Teaching mode when the user asks to understand how something works.
---

## Workflow

1. Start with the real thing the user is looking at: code, docs, runtime evidence, product behavior, or their stated situation. Explain what matters,
   what must stay true, what can go wrong, and why this choice fits here.
2. Help the user connect the explanation to the project by using its real names, flows, files, callers, constraints, history, and evidence. Add
   examples, counterexamples, or trade-offs when they make the idea clearer.
3. Take it step by step when several ideas depend on one another. Even when the implementation is simple, share the reasoning that makes the choice
   clear.
