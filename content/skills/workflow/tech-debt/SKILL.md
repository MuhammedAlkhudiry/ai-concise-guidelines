## Workflow

1. Capture intentionally deferred work in the project's TECH_DEBT.md, create or update.
2. Use one unchecked task per item. State the deferred action, why it is deferred, and point to the relevant code, component, or decision when useful, versioning (like the last supported mobile app version), and cleanup steps.
3. Remove item when the work is resolved.
