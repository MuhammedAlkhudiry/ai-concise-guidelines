# Improve Activation

Improve the system that gets new or invited users from intent to durable first value.

## Workflow

1. Use `product-setup` for durable product context and approved evidence sources.
2. Identify the target segment, entry path, promised value, activation event, denominator, and time window.
3. Trace the path from acquisition promise, signup or invite, setup, first value, confirmation, and first return.
4. Inspect product screens, routes, code, analytics events, lifecycle messages, docs, tests, support clues, and fixtures.
5. Map every required user action, system dependency, wait state, empty state, permission, import, default, and decision point.
6. Find where users lose intent, confidence, momentum, data, proof of value, or a clear next step.
7. Separate observed evidence from assumptions and name instrumentation gaps directly.
8. Recommend changes across UX, defaults, sample data, templates, lifecycle messaging, analytics, and human-assisted paths.
9. Prioritize by activation leverage, reach, confidence, implementation size, and risk.
10. Include the full recommended activation scope unless the user asks for an MVP or staged rollout.

## Audit Surface

- Acquisition promise and expectation mismatch.
- Signup, invite, account setup, role, team, and permissions.
- First-run screens, empty states, setup tasks, demo data, templates, imports, and integrations.
- Aha moment, activation event, time-to-value, and early success confirmation.
- Dropoff recovery through in-app messages, email, reminders, support, or founder-led onboarding.
- Monetization timing and first-return loops after activation.

## Output

- Activation definition and evidence quality.
- Current path summary.
- Main activation blockers.
- Recommended product, UX, messaging, analytics, and operational changes, with priority rationale.

## Rules
- Optimize for activation leverage, not smallness.
- Do not confuse onboarding completion with activation.
- Do not recommend tours, checklists, emails, or polish unless they move users toward first value.
- Prefer removing required steps over explaining them better.
- If the product has multiple user types, separate activation paths by segment.
