# Channel And Content Ops

Use this reference for organic social, content calendars, creative briefs, landing copy, SEO/discovery content, email/SMS drafts, and asset repurposing.

## Content Workflow

1. Read project setup and real assets before drafting.
2. Pick the job of the content: awareness, trust, proof, education, lead capture, conversion, retention, or reactivation.
3. Match the channel:
   - Social: native hook, asset, caption, CTA, hashtags/keywords, alt text, and reply plan.
   - Website/landing: intent, promise, proof, objections, conversion path, and tracking.
   - SEO/discovery: query intent, page type, internal links, schema/content requirements, and freshness.
   - Email/SMS: segment, trigger, subject/preview, message, CTA, compliance, and unsubscribe expectations.
4. Draft multiple options only when the choices are meaningfully different.
5. Verify against brand voice, claims, product facts, and approval rules.

## Content Calendar Shape

- Content pillars.
- Target channel.
- Asset/source.
- Draft copy.
- CTA.
- Owner or approval needed.
- Publish window.
- Measurement: what would make this content worth repeating?

## Draft Quality Checks

- Use real product/service details and real proof.
- Avoid fake urgency, unverifiable claims, and generic motivational filler.
- Localize language, cultural references, and platform tone.
- Keep the next action clear.
- For public posts, include an approval checkpoint before publishing.
