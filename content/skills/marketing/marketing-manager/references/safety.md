# Marketing Safety

Use this reference before public, paid, outreach, account, tracking, or compliance-sensitive work.

## Approval Required

Require explicit approval before:

- Publishing, scheduling, editing, deleting, or pinning public content.
- Sending DMs, public replies, comments, email, SMS, WhatsApp messages, or lead outreach.
- Creating, launching, pausing, deleting, or editing ads, campaigns, ad groups, ad sets, keywords, targeting, audiences, budgets, bids, feeds, forms, pixels, tags, conversion actions, domains, billing, verification, or account settings.
- Running broad automation that may trigger platform rate limits or spam enforcement.

## Sensitive Data

- Do not print or store tokens, cookies, private keys, API secrets, payment details, full customer records, private DMs, raw lead exports, bearer headers, or full connection strings.
- Summarize private messages and customer data unless the user explicitly asks for targeted review.
- Redact account numbers when they are not necessary for execution.

## Platform And Brand Risk

- Check current platform policy when automation, scraping, ads claims, restricted categories, political/financial/health/legal claims, or targeting rules matter.
- Avoid misleading claims, fake scarcity, fabricated testimonials, unverifiable performance claims, and impersonation.
- For lead outreach, draft helpful context-aware replies and avoid repetitive unsolicited posting.

## Approval Prompt Shape

When asking for approval, include:

- Account/channel.
- Exact action.
- Exact content/settings.
- Expected result.
- Known risks.
- What cannot be easily undone.
